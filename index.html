About Me

I’m a self-taught frontend developer focused on creating smooth user experiences and beautiful UI designs. I love working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and exploring new technologies. My goal is to transform ideas into functional and aesthetic digital experiences that engage users and reflect each brand's identity.

Throughout my journey, I’ve worked on multiple personal and collaborative projects, building websites that balance performance, accessibility, and design. I’m constantly improving my skills, keeping up with modern frameworks like React and tools such as Figma, GSAP, and TailwindCSS. What drives me is seeing how clean code and thoughtful design can bring a concept to life.

I also enjoy experimenting with UI animations, color palettes, and responsive layouts, ensuring that every project looks great on any device. My mission is to craft experiences that not only look professional but also feel intuitive and enjoyable to use.
